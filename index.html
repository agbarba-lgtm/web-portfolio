Portfolio Showcase

Project

Canlubang, Calamba City, Laguna

Paikit Health Center

Paikit Health Center is a community health center in Canlubang, Calamba City, Laguna, and this web-based system was built to bring its services closer to the people it serves. Patients around Canlubang can book a consultation online from their own homes, without having to travel to the center just to set an appointment. By making scheduling simpler and more convenient, the project aims to save residents time, reduce crowding and long waits at the center, and help the health center's staff manage consultations in a more organized way.
